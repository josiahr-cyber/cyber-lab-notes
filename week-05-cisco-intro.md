# Cisco Intro to Cybersecurity, Module 1.1
Cisco Networking Academy, Oct 11, 2026

## What it covered
The World of Cybersecurity: what cybersecurity is, protecting personal data, online identity, smart devices, what hackers want, and identity theft.

## What I learned
- Data is valuable for several reasons. One main reason is that it can be the gateway to your finances, which hackers can't get enough of.
- A great way to protect personal data and avoid identity theft is to watch who you share information with and what medium you use to share it.
- Even simple things like usernames can be a security risk, and so can pictures I send to "trusted friends."

## What I'll do differently
One habit I will change is being careful about what is on my screen when I share it with a friend.
